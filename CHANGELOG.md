# Glance Platform 2.0.4

- Included package versions: Finance 2.0.1, Usage 2.0.0, Plex 2.0.1, Investments 2.0.1, Weather 2.0.1, Clock 2.0.0, Notes 2.0.0, Liquid Icons 2.0.0, Storage 2.0.0, System Resources 2.0.0, Cameras 2.0.3, SABnzbd 2.0.0, Messages 2.0.0 and qBittorrent 2.0.0.
- **Custom desktops: outlines turn their corners cleanly.** Where merged tiles surround an empty space, small corner pieces round the outline's inside corners. A piece made before a custom desktop showed stayed behind the desktop's background, so the outline faded out at that corner and looked darker there. The pieces now come in front with their tiles (and are checked every second).
- **Custom desktops: no more tiles stuck behind the background.** Right after Glance starts (for example after an update), Finance shows its charts one at a time, and a desktop moved on after the first one: the other charts came up in their right places but behind the desktop's background, so only one showed. A desktop now waits until a tile has as many windows as it has spots there, and any window of its tiles that shows up later (right away, or while the desktop shows: checked every second) goes to its spot and in front. Nothing saved was lost; the spots were always kept.
- **Cameras 2.0.3: turn your cameras from the tile.** A camera that can turn (pan and tilt, like the Tapo C500) shows four arrows at the bottom of its picture when the pointer is over it: hold one to turn the camera, let go to stop, as in the Tapo app. It works in a camera's own tile, in a shared tile and at full screen. Glance asks each camera through ONVIF (the standard camera-control language; Tapo cameras answer on port 2020) with the camera account you already entered, sent as a password digest; cameras that can't turn (a doorbell) show no arrows. **Right-drag like a joystick:** hold the right mouse button on a picture and drag; the camera turns that way, diagonals too, faster the farther you drag, and stops when you let go (a ring and a line show the drag; a right-click without dragging still opens the menu). **Home point:** the house with a plus at the end of the row (or right-click → Tile actions → **Set home point here**) saves where the camera points now (a saved position named "Glance home" on the camera; saving again replaces it; a check mark shows for a moment), and the house between the arrows (or **Go to home point**) turns it back there; it stays dimmed until a home point is saved. **Zoom with the mouse wheel:** turn the wheel over a camera's picture to zoom in toward the pointer, up to 8× (the picture only; a label shows how far); while zoomed in, drag the picture to look around (also full screen); zoom all the way out, or right-click → Tile actions → **Zoom all the way out**, and dragging moves the tile again. A zoomed-in camera uses its full-quality video when Video quality is Auto.
- **Custom desktops keep each Finance chart in its own spot.** A desktop used to move all of a Finance tile's charts as one block, so one chart out of place shifted the whole row, and they drifted from swap to swap. Each chart now carries its own number (like each camera window), so Save arrangement keeps every chart's spot and size and the desktop puts each one back exactly (Finance 2.0.1). After updating, show the desktop, place the charts once and click **Save arrangement**.
- **Every tile's border line looks the same.** The faint (Subtle) border was drawn at each tile's zoom and fell between screen pixels, so it came out two pixels on the top and left, one on the bottom and right, and fainter still on some tiles (Plex). Borders are now drawn on whole screen pixels, the same on every side of every tile: Subtle as the lightest of those lines (two pixels at half strength), Accent and Custom solid at the chosen thickness. All tiles use Glance's drawing for this, so no tile needs an update.
- **Custom desktops keep every window on the screen.** A tile or program whose place on a desktop would stick out past the screen's edge (for example a Finance chart moved with the others as one block, from an arrangement saved before 2.0.1) is slid back onto the screen it covers most, keeping its size. A few pixels over an edge, or a window across two monitors of an all-displays desktop, stay as they are.
- **Restore saved works while a custom desktop shows.** It puts that desktop's tiles and programs back where Save arrangement left them (before, it only asked you to go back to your normal desktop first); your normal desktop's places stay as they were.
- **Custom desktops: every tile appears together with the swap.** Before the swap picture lifts, Glance now asks every tile window to finish painting (waiting at most 0.6 s), so slow-drawing tiles such as Finance's charts no longer show up after the rest. The swap log lists it as "painted N".

# Glance Platform 2.0.0

- Included package versions: Finance 2.0.0, Usage 2.0.0, Plex 2.0.1, Investments 2.0.1, Weather 2.0.1, Clock 2.0.0, Notes 2.0.0, Liquid Icons 2.0.0, Storage 2.0.0, System Resources 2.0.0, Cameras 2.0.0, SABnzbd 2.0.0, Messages 2.0.0 and qBittorrent 2.0.0.
- **Plex, Weather and Investments shrink narrower** (to 200, 260 and 300 wide; Plex 2.0.1 leaves out its temperatures when narrow, Weather 2.0.1, Investments 2.0.1).
- **Every bundled tile is 2.0.0** (Cameras, Clock, Finance, Investments, Liquid Icons, LLM Usage, Messages, Notes, Plex, qBittorrent, SABnzbd, Storage, System Resources, Weather), so each one updates on installed PCs with this release.
- **Glance 2.0: every tile resizes freely.** Drag any side or corner of any tile to give it the size and shape you want, so anything fits a custom desktop. System Resources, Storage, Weather, Investments, Plex and LLM Usage used to keep one shape and only zoom; now they lay their content out to the size given: bars and text follow the width, rows spread out on a taller tile, Weather shows fewer days (at least three) on a shorter one, Investments as many holdings as fit (no 10-row limit), Plex as many viewers as fit (the rest scroll), and a wide Storage tile also shows the percent used. Each keeps a smallest size where everything stays whole. **Hold Ctrl while dragging a corner** to zoom instead (text and pictures grow or shrink with the tile, keeping its shape); the Size setting in Style still works. Right-click → **Size to fit** gives such a tile the size its content needs. Finance's slim Mini bar now stretches sideways too.
- **Cameras: any shape.** A camera's tile no longer keeps the camera's shape while you resize it. Cameras Options → Picture: **Whole picture** (black bars where the box is a different shape; the default) or **Fill the box** (the edges are cut off). Right-click → Tile actions → **Fit to the camera's picture** puts a camera's tile back in its camera's shape.
- **New look for every menu.** All menus in Glance and its tiles now share one look: Windows 11's rounded corners (square on Windows 10), taller rows, a rounded highlight under the pointer, slim separators, a thin arrow for submenus and the lavender check mark. That covers tile right-click menus, Glance's own menus and tray menu, SABnzbd's download menus, qBittorrent's page menus (styled to match), and the choice menus in Plex, LLM Usage and Finance Options (Finance's were plain Windows menus). The look lives in the tile kit (`TileMenus`), so other people's tiles can use it too.
- **Cameras decode video on the graphics card.** Measured on a PC with two camera windows of about 1000 x 587 at full quality: the Cameras tile used about 1.4 processor cores, nearly all of it Windows decoding the video in software (every other tile used under 0.1% of the processor). Windows' own decoder now gets a Direct3D 11 device and decodes on the graphics card when the PC can; if it can't, or stops partway, the camera goes on decoding on the processor. Cameras Options → **Video decoding**: on the graphics card when this PC can (the default) or on the processor only. The tile's status in Glance says "· graphics card" when every camera decodes there.
- **qBittorrent rests while hidden.** Hidden (Hide tile, or put away by a custom desktop), its page no longer asks qBittorrent for news every second and a half, and it uses less memory (it took about 400 MB); it wakes at once when shown. (SABnzbd, System Resources, Liquid Icons and Cameras already paused while hidden; Messages stays awake so new-message alerts still come.)
- **Start-up time in the log.** Each start notes in glance.log how long Glance's window took to appear and every tile to start, with the slowest three (`[glance.platform] Started: …`), so start-up can be compared from version to version.
- **Desktop tab: your normal desktop is saved like a custom desktop.** **Save arrangement** now keeps, for every tile, its place and size, lock, whether it is hidden, its zoom, and each camera window's spot; **Restore saved** puts all of that back (on screen). Arrangements saved before still restore their places, sizes and locks. **Arrange tiles** is gone: it piled every tile in rows on the main screen, and custom desktops do that job now. While a custom desktop shows, Save arrangement still saves that desktop.
- **Upgrades from Glance 1.6 and newer only.** Code that converted settings from before 1.6 was removed (the cleanup of the retired Voya import in Investments, Liquid Icons' rim settings from before 1.3.5, and `tools/Import-LegacySettings.ps1`, the one-time import from the standalone apps).
- **No more hover pop-ups on the tiles themselves** (they covered the screen): System Resources, Storage, Weather, Investments and Plex lost theirs, like SABnzbd, qBittorrent and Cameras before. What they said is on the tile, in its status in Glance, or in its Options.
- **2.0 cleanup (no visible change):** one window snapping module in the tile kit (there were three copies), one safe store for passwords and keys (Windows' per-user encryption; Cameras, SABnzbd, Plex and Tdarr each had their own), one set of fonts per tile, the title-bar buttons of SABnzbd, qBittorrent and Messages shared, the web-page code of Messages and qBittorrent shared (`examples/Shared`), the Storage and System Resources rows shared (`TileRows` in the kit), and one build script and one check script for every bundled tile (`tools/Tiles.ps1`, `tools/Build-Tile.ps1`, `tools/Test-Tile.ps1` replace fourteen scripts).

- **Cameras 1.4.0: large camera windows as smooth as full screen, a sharper picture, and full screen no longer forced on top.** A camera window resized large (to fill a monitor) still lagged: each new picture repainted its whole box with smoothed rounded shapes (the box, its card, the header) before the picture, which full screen never did; now a new picture repaints only the picture, copied in one step, with the rounded corners put back in small squares at the corners. Pictures shown larger or smaller (less than half) now blend the four nearest brightness samples instead of taking the nearest one, so a camera shown large is smooth instead of blocky (shrinking to half or less still averages). Double-click full screen is no longer forced on top of everything: Glance (to save a desktop's arrangement) and other windows can come in front, and Always on top still applies when it is set. A camera window moved or resized from outside (a custom desktop putting it in place, or back) now saves where it is, like after your own drag, so a camera no longer opens at a desktop's spot after the next start.
- **Hidden tiles stay hidden after an update or a restart.** Glance never saved which tiles were hidden, so every start (each update) brought them all back on your normal desktop. Now a tile hidden or shown on your normal desktop is remembered (the tile's own Hide tile and Esc, Show tile and Hide tile in My Tiles, Show all and Hide all; saved as `Hidden` on the tile) and it starts hidden; turning a tile on, Show tile and Bring tiles onscreen show it again. Hiding a tile on a showing custom desktop stays that desktop's own (hidden there only), as before.
- **Custom desktops: tiles that size themselves keep their own size, and every zooming tile its own zoom per desktop.** Plex, LLM Usage, Storage, System Resources, Weather and Investments size themselves to what they show; a custom desktop now only moves them (it used to store and force a window size, which left Plex at a size it no longer had). Their size on each desktop is their zoom: Save arrangement keeps it, and it is set the way the tile's own corner-drag sets it (Plex and LLM Usage keep their zoom inside the tile, which desktops couldn't see before; each tile window now shows its zoom, `Glance.Window.Zoom`, and Glance can ask a tile for a zoom). Going back gives each its own zoom and place back.
- **Cameras 1.3.0: no more lag at full screen.** A camera shown large (full screen, or a big window) fell further and further behind and made the tile slow: one thread per camera read the video, decoded it and then made each picture at the size shown with a plain loop, which took about 60 ms for a full-screen picture, more than the time between two frames, so reading the camera waited and the delay grew. Now pictures are made by a second thread per camera from the newest decoded frame only (a frame that arrives while one is being made replaces the waiting one, so the video stays live), straight into the picture's memory and in bands on all cores (about 3 ms for a full-screen picture on the owner's PC, the same pixels as before), and drawn as a Windows bitmap copied to the screen in one step instead of through GDI+ (the rounded corners are painted after the picture instead of clipping each one). A camera's new picture repaints only that camera's box, not every camera window. Checks compare the new pictures with the old loop pixel for pixel. (1.2.0, only in test build 1.7.13: each camera's window carries a lasting number for its camera, the window property `Glance.Window.Key`, so Glance's custom desktops can give each camera its own spot and size.)
- **Custom desktops on all displays.** A desktop's Monitor can now be **All displays** (desktop editor → Monitor, the last choice when there are two or more monitors): one shortcut covers every monitor with the desktop's background and puts its tiles and programs on any of them, for example the security cameras across both screens. Spots are measured from the top left of all the monitors; switching a desktop to or from All displays keeps what is arranged where it is on the screen. Showing it puts away a desktop showing on any of its monitors; other tiles on any monitor are put away while it shows. Saved with the desktop (`AllMonitors`).
- **Custom desktops: each camera window keeps its own spot and size.** A tile with several windows that say which one they are (the Cameras tile's cameras, each in its own window) gets each window's own spot and size on each desktop: Save arrangement records them (`Spots` on the member), the next show puts each camera back there, and going back puts each where it was on your normal desktop. Other tiles with several windows (Finance charts) keep moving together as before.
- **Custom desktops swap faster.** Tiles hidden on your normal desktop that show on a custom desktop are now waited for together instead of one after another (Desktop 1 with seven of them took 0.65 to 0.8 s just for that), and every tile's window is sent to its place at the same time (each tile moves its own while the others do), then all come to the front; going back sends them back the same way. The log's step times now include "in place" and "sent back".
- **Custom desktops swap all at once, and faster.** The moment a desktop's shortcut is pressed (or Show desktop, Go back), a still picture of the screens the swap changes covers them; every window moves, shows and hides behind it, and the picture goes when everything is in place, so the new arrangement appears in one step instead of one window after another (the background first, then each tile). The picture never takes focus or clicks and is gone a moment after the swap. **No white flash**: the picture is given to Windows before its window appears (a window that draws itself after it appears is plain white for a moment, which flashed on both monitors in test build 1.7.11), every monitor is covered (a tile hidden on your normal desktop shows for a moment at its own place, which may be on the other monitor, before it moves onto the desktop), and the screens are copied at the same time (about 30 ms each). The swap itself is quicker: tiles to show or put away are told first and answer while the other windows are placed, and the waits for them are shorter (at most 0.7 to 1.2 s, usually a few hundredths). How long each swap took goes to Glance's log (`glance.log`: "Desktop 1 shown in … ms"), with a note when a tile was slow to show or hide.
- **Custom desktops: each desktop is set up on its own.** While a custom desktop shows, only its own tiles are on its monitor: other tiles sitting on that monitor are put away (hidden) and come back exactly where they were when you go back (they used to stay behind the desktop's background, or in front of it when set to Always on top). **Hiding a tile on a custom desktop hides it on that desktop only** (right-click → Hide tile, Hide all, or Esc on the tile): going back puts it on your normal desktop as it was, and the next time that desktop shows the tile stays off it. Its editor marks it "(hidden on this desktop)", and **Show here** (the Bring to front button for such a tile; also Show tile or Show all from Glance while the desktop shows) puts it back on. **A tile hidden on your normal desktop still shows on a custom desktop it's on**, and is hidden again when you go back. **Each tile keeps its own size there**: Save arrangement now also records a one-window tile's size and zoom on that desktop, and your normal desktop keeps its own (a tile whose zoom differs gets its zoom back through the tile, the way its corner-drag sets it). A tile with windows on two monitors only has the windows on the desktop's monitor put away. Hidden on a desktop is saved with the desktop (`Hidden`, `Zoom` on each member).
- **Custom desktops: the Desktop tab follows the desktop you're looking at.** Its map draws a showing custom desktop with its background color and name, and only that desktop's tiles on its monitor (the tiles put away behind it are no longer drawn), with a line above the map saying which desktop shows where; without one it says "Your normal desktop". **Save arrangement** there saves the showing custom desktop's arrangement (your normal desktop's saved arrangement is left alone); Arrange tiles, Restore saved and Bring tiles onscreen, which put tiles at their normal places, ask you to go back first. Show tile (My Tiles) for a tile put away explains why it isn't there and how to add it to the desktop.
- **Custom desktops: tiles and programs always come in front of the desktop's background.** Pressing a desktop's shortcut moved its tiles to their spots, but a tile could stay hidden behind the background: Windows ignores a request to bring a window to the very top when it comes from a program that isn't the one in front. Each window now goes to the front instead (right behind the window you are using, which Windows allows): in front of the background and in front of the desktop's other windows, so a tile added while the desktop shows lands on top, ready to drag where it goes.
- Custom desktops: **Bring to front** in a desktop's editor (or double-click a tile or program in its list) brings that one forward while the desktop shows, for one hidden behind another.
- Custom desktops: shortcuts read as printed on the keys, for example **Ctrl + =** instead of Windows' name "Ctrl + Oemplus" (punctuation keys follow the keyboard layout in use).
